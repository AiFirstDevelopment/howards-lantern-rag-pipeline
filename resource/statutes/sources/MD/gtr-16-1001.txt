Article - Transportation
§16–1001.
(a) In this subtitle the following words have the meanings indicated.
(b) “Credential holder” means the individual to whom an electronic credential is issued.
(c) “Data field” means a discrete piece of information printed or otherwise appearing on a license or an identification card.
(d) “Electronic credential” means an electronic representation of a license, an identification card, or a data field.
(e) “Full profile” means all the information provided on a license or an identification card.
(f) “Limited profile” means a portion of the information provided on a license or an identification card.
