Article - Transportation
§11–103.5.
“Autocycle” means a motor vehicle that:
(1) Has two front wheels and one rear wheel;
(2) Has a steering mechanism;
(3) Has permanent seats on which the operator or a passenger is not required to sit astride;
(4) Has foot pedals to control at least one of the following:
(i) Acceleration;
(ii) Braking; or
(iii) If applicable, a clutch; and
(5) Is manufactured to comply with federal safety standards for motorcycles.
