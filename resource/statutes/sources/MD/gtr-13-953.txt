Article - Transportation
§13–953.
(a) Any person with a perfected security interest in a vehicle may apply to the Administration for a duplicate of the security interest filing.
(b) If a person who applies to the Administration for a duplicate of the security interest filing under this section furnishes information satisfactory to the Administration and pays the required fee, the Administration shall issue to the person a duplicate of the security interest filing.
(c) For the issuance of a duplicate of a security interest filing under this section, the fee shall be established by the Administration.
