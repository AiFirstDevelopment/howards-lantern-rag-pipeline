Article - Transportation
§16–107.
(a) The application of a minor for a license shall be cosigned by:
(1) A parent or guardian of the applicant;
(2) If the applicant has no parent or guardian or is married, an adult employer of the applicant or any other responsible adult; or
(3) If the applicant is committed to the custody or guardianship of a local department of social services, the director of the department or the director’s designee.
(b) The individual cosigning the application of a minor shall:
(1) (i) Provide the cosigner’s mailing address to the Administration; and
(ii) Within 30 days of any change in the mailing address occurring while the applicant or licensee is a minor, notify the Administration of the change; and
(2) Certify that the statements made in the application are true to the best of the cosigner’s knowledge, information, and belief.
