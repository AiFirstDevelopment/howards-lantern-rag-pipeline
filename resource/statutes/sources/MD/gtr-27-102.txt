Article - Transportation
§27–102.
Except as provided in § 21–1207.1 of this article, a person who violates a restriction imposed on any license under the Maryland Vehicle Law or who violates a regulation adopted under the Maryland Vehicle Law is guilty of a misdemeanor and on conviction, in addition to any administrative penalty provided in the Maryland Vehicle Law, is subject to the greater of:
(1) The penalty provided in § 27–101(b) of this title;
(2) The penalty provided for a violation of § 16–113(j) of this article; or
(3) The penalty provided for a violation of the statute under which the restriction is imposed or the regulation adopted.
