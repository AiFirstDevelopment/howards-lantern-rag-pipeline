Article - Transportation
§21–807.
In each charge of a violation of any speed regulation under the Maryland Vehicle Law, the charging document shall specify:
(1) The speed at which the defendant is alleged to have driven;
(2) If the charge is for exceeding a maximum lawful speed, the maximum speed limit applicable at the location; and
(3) If the charge is for driving below a minimum lawful speed, the minimum speed limit applicable at the location.
