Article - Criminal Law
§10–109.
(a) A person may not place or allow to be placed outside of a building or dwelling an abandoned or discarded refrigerator, icebox, or freezer cabinet that:
(1) is in a place accessible to children;
(2) is uncrated; and
(3) has a door or a lock that cannot be released for opening from the inside.
(b) A person who violates this section is guilty of a misdemeanor and on conviction is subject to imprisonment not exceeding 30 days or a fine not exceeding $100 or both.
