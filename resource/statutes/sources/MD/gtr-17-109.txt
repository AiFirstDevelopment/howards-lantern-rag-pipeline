Article - Transportation
§17–109.
(a) A person who receives a warning letter under Title 16, Subtitle 4 of this article as a result of point accumulation shall submit evidence that any vehicle registered in the person’s name, individually or jointly, has been continuously covered, since the notice date of the point accumulation warning letter, by the security required under this subtitle.
(b) The evidence of security shall be submitted to the Administration within 30 days of the request on a form prescribed by the Administration and certified by an insurer or insurance producer.
