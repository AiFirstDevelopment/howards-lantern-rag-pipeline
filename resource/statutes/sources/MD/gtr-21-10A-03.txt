Article - Transportation
§21–10A–03.
(a) A vehicle may not be towed or otherwise removed from a parking lot to a location that is:
(1) Subject to subsection (b) of this section, more than 15 miles from the parking lot; or
(2) Outside the State.
(b) A local jurisdiction may establish a maximum distance from a parking lot to a towed vehicle storage facility that is different than that established under subsection (a)(1) of this section.
