Article - Transportation
§11–143.
“Owner”, as used in reference to a vehicle:
(1) Means a person who has the property in or title to the vehicle;
(2) Includes a person who, subject to a security interest in another person, is entitled to the use and possession of the vehicle;
(3) Does not include a lessee under a lease not intended as security; and
(4) Includes a lessee under a lease intended as a security.
