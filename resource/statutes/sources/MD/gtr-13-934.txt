Article - Transportation
§13–934.
(a) When registered with the Administration, every vehicle used as a vanpool vehicle is a Class J (vanpool) vehicle.
(b) For each Class J (vanpool) vehicle, the annual registration fee is:
(1) On or after July 1, 2024, but before July 1, 2025, $96.50; and
(2) On or after July 1, 2025, $106.50.
