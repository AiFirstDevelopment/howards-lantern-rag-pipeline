Article - Transportation
§11–134.4.
(a) “Motorized minibike” means a motor vehicle that:
(1) Has two or three wheels; and
(2) Is not subject to registration under Title 13 of this article.
(b) “Motorized minibike” does not include:
(1) A motor scooter;
(2) A moped;
(3) A farm tractor;
(4) An electric bicycle; or
(5) An electric low speed scooter.
