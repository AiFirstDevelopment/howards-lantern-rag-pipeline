Article - Transportation
§13–607.
(a) A person may not issue any temporary registration plate that contains any misstatement of fact.
(b) A person may not knowingly insert any false information on the face of a temporary registration plate.
(c) A dealer may not issue a temporary registration plate to any person who has annual registration plates for a vehicle that has been sold or exchanged.
(d) A dealer may not lend a temporary registration plate to any person.
(e) A dealer may not use a temporary registration plate on any vehicle that the dealer owns.
(f) A dealer may not otherwise issue, assign, transfer, or deliver any temporary registration plate to any person for any reason except as authorized by this part.
